# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Home gardeners (yard, balcony, or raised bed) with little to no gardening experience, planning a season-long garden. They use the app to get a personalized crop plan, track planting dates/care tasks, and check in on their gardens over the course of a growing season — a recurring, low-stakes utility visit rather than a one-time flow.

### Brand Personality
Clean, minimal, calm — Apple/Notion-like restraint applied to a garden-planning app. Approachable and encouraging (garden growth is a season-long journey, not a race), never twee or cartoonish. Content and whitespace carry the design; color is used sparingly as an accent, not a backdrop.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light mode only for now (matches rest of the app).
- Typography: system/Apple-native font stack (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Text", "SF Pro Display"`) with Inter as the cross-platform fallback — not Inter-first.
- Color: existing `sprout` green scale stays the accent color; neutrals are slate. Keep pure grays tinted, avoid flat gray-on-white blandness.
- Shape language: rounded-2xl cards, rounded-full buttons (already established on the landing page) — carry this through consistently.
- No dark mode, no glassmorphism, no gradient text, no decorative sparklines/icons above every heading.
- Anti-reference: generic "AI dashboard" look — identical icon+heading+text card grids, hero-metric-with-gradient tiles, everything centered.

### Design Principles
1. Whitespace and type hierarchy do the work before color or borders do.
2. Left-align content and break from a perfectly centered, everything-in-a-card layout — group by proximity, not by wrapping everything in `.card`.
3. One clear primary action per view; secondary actions stay visually quiet (ghost/text style).
4. Reuse the landing page's established visual language (sprout green accent, rounded-full CTAs, rounded-2xl surfaces) rather than inventing a new one for app pages.
5. Empty and loading states should feel considered (real copy, real structure) rather than a bare "Loading…" string.
